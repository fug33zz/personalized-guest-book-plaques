# Wedding design research

Research was performed against public wedding stationery and guest-book search pages in August 2026. Etsy and Zazzle blocked automated catalogue access, and Pinterest did not expose public save counts in its page response. Zola exposed 134 catalogue descriptions but no item-level rating or review totals.

The resulting counts are prevalence signals, not sales rankings. Categories overlap because one design can be both botanical and classic.

| Recurring language | Matching Zola catalogue descriptions |
| --- | ---: |
| Floral, botanical, foliage, blossom, or garden | 72 |
| Border or frame | 49 |
| Classic, timeless, or traditional | 38 |
| Modern, editorial, architectural, or contemporary | 25 |
| Romantic, calligraphy, or flowing script | 24 |
| Minimalist, minimal, clean, or less-is-more | 21 |

Source: https://www.zola.com/wedding-planning/invitations/shop

## Product interpretation

Photo-led designs do not translate to a two-colour raised plaque. Monogram-led and hard editorial concepts were also excluded after direct product feedback. The implemented collection therefore prioritizes botanical, framed, timeless, romantic, minimalist, and vintage styles, with softer arch, scallop, cameo, and geometric variations suitable for printable relief.

No marketplace artwork was copied. The collection applies recurring composition principles through original, shared geometric primitives.
